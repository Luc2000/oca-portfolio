## Who we are

**OCA Software House** (https://oca.dev.br) is a software house in São Paulo, Brazil,
run by a small senior team. We design, build, and operate digital products end to end:
mobile apps, web systems, and AI-powered products.

We are builders first. Our own product, **Revo App**, is a complete event discovery
and ticketing platform with **40,000+ registered users**, **300+ events per month**,
and a checkout that converts at **90%**. We use those production numbers as proof:
we don't sell theory, we sell what we already do for ourselves.

Selected portfolio (all real, most in production):

- **Revo App** — event platform, 40k+ users, own checkout with 90% conversion (React Native, Supabase, Pagar.me).
- **VoxField** — AI voice agent for phone surveys, piloting with a real client.
- **REX** — AI sales agent that goes from lead search to outbound message autonomously.
- **Delta Stats** — statistical analysis product for traders, sold by subscription.
- **Vipou** — real-time event management.
- **Meyu** — interactive digital menu.
- **Gestão para Imobiliárias** — end-to-end rental operations system, in production.

Services: custom mobile apps (React Native/Expo), web systems (Next.js/React/Node),
MVPs for startups, and applied AI (chatbots, AI agents, process automation).

The blog exists to attract two audiences from Google and AI assistants and route
them to a WhatsApp conversation with the OCA team.
**The blog is not the destination, it is the path.**

## The demand machine and the partner network

OCA ranks on Google and is cited by AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity) for
software development queries in Brazil. That visibility brings **dozens of client
leads every day, more than the team can serve**. OCA's answer is a **partner network**:
trusted senior devs and small teams that receive vetted, scoped projects handed off
by OCA.

This matters for content in two ways:

- For **client posts**, the inbound volume is social proof: OCA is the team other
  people already found and trusted.
- For **dev posts**, it is the core narrative: the same SEO machine the dev is reading
  right now is the one generating more projects than OCA can handle. The dev can both
  learn the recipe and join the network to receive the overflow.

## Audiences in detail

### Clientes — the person with an idea
- Non-technical founders and professionals who had a product idea and don't know where to start
- People googling "quanto custa criar um aplicativo", "como criar um app", "quanto custa um mvp"
- First-time entrepreneurs afraid of being ripped off by dev shops
- Corporate employees planning a side product or startup
- They need honesty about cost, timeline, and scope more than they need jargon

### Clientes — the company that needs software
- Small and mid-sized companies whose operation outgrew spreadsheets and WhatsApp
- Businesses wanting AI in customer service or internal processes but unsure what is hype
- Companies burned by a previous bad development experience (frozen project, no maintenance)
- Startups needing a senior team to build or rescue their product
- Decision makers comparing off-the-shelf SaaS vs custom software

### Devs — the future partner network
- Brazilian devs who deliver well but have no client pipeline of their own
- CLT developers doing the math on going freelance or PJ
- Freelancers tired of race-to-the-bottom pricing on gig platforms
- Small dev teams that could absorb handed-off projects
- They respect competence and hate guru content; talk to them like peers

## Brand pillars (use these to give answers authority)

- **Built by builders** — we run our own product with 40k+ users; every recommendation
  comes from something we operate in production, not from a slide deck.
- **Senior team only** — no juniors learning on the client's budget. Small team,
  direct access to the people who actually write the code.
- **End to end** — strategy, design, development, deployment, and growth. One team
  accountable for the whole product.
- **Honest scope** — we tell clients what NOT to build. MVPs start small on purpose.
  For projects with market potential we are open to partnerships beyond development.
